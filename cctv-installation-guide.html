CCTV Installation Guide for Businesses

July 29, 2026   Admin

Back to Blog

A business CCTV system should be planned around coverage, image quality, storage, privacy and reliable network connectivity. Camera placement and recording requirements should be decided before installation begins.

Plan Camera Locations

Identify entrances, exits, reception areas, corridors, parking areas and other locations where surveillance is required.

Choose the Right Camera Type

Dome, bullet, turret and other camera designs suit different mounting and coverage requirements.

Consider Lighting

Select cameras suitable for the available lighting and consider night vision or appropriate illumination where needed.

Plan Storage

Estimate recording retention, camera resolution, frame rate and the number of cameras before selecting storage capacity.

Secure the CCTV Network

Change default credentials, update camera and recorder firmware and restrict remote access.

Test the System

After installation, verify camera angles, recording, playback, notifications and backup power where applicable.

Maintain the System

Clean lenses, check storage health, review firmware and periodically confirm that all cameras are recording correctly.
