PRIVACY POLICY

Your Data Security Is Our Priority

Technova IT Solutions respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your business and personal information.

Introduction

At Technova IT Solutions, we understand the importance of protecting customer information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect information provided by our clients and website visitors.

Information We Collect

We may collect information such as:

  • Name, company details and contact information
  • Email address and phone number
  • IT support requirements and service details
  • Technical information required for providing services

How We Use Your Information

Technova IT Solutions uses collected information only for providing better IT services, technical support and improving customer experience.

  • Providing IT infrastructure support and maintenance.
  • Responding to customer enquiries and service requests.
  • Managing AMC contracts and technical assistance.
  • Improving our services, security and customer support.

Remote Support & Technical Data Protection

During remote support sessions, our engineers may access customer systems only with customer approval. We follow strict confidentiality practices while troubleshooting and maintaining IT systems.

  • Remote access is performed only after client permission.
  • Customer files and business data are not copied or shared.
  • Access credentials are handled securely.
  • Sessions are closed after completion of support work.

Confidentiality Commitment

Technova IT Solutions maintains strict confidentiality for all customer information including network details, server configurations, business applications and technical documentation. We do not sell, rent or share customer information with unauthorized third parties.

Security Practices

We follow professional security practices to protect customer information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

  • Secure handling of customer information.
  • Professional access control procedures.
  • Network and system security best practices.
  • Regular improvement of security processes.

Third Party Services

Our website may use trusted third-party services such as analytics, hosting providers or communication services to improve website performance and user experience. These third-party providers have their own privacy policies and security practices.

Cookies Policy

Our website may use cookies to improve browsing experience, understand website usage and provide better services. Users can control or disable cookies through their browser settings.

Data Retention

We retain customer information only for as long as required to provide services, maintain records and fulfill business requirements.

Your Rights

Customers have the right to request information about their data, ask for corrections or request removal of information where applicable.

  • Request details about collected information.
  • Update incorrect contact information.
  • Ask questions regarding privacy practices.
  • Request assistance related to data protection.

Children's Privacy

Technova IT Solutions does not intentionally collect personal information from children. Our services are designed for businesses and professional users.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology or legal requirements. Visitors are encouraged to review this page periodically for the latest information.
